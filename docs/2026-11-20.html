Myanmar committed genocide against Rohingya minority, world’s top court rules

世界最高法院裁定緬甸對羅興亞少數族群犯下種族滅絕罪
來源 The Guardian World ↗
Summary
The International Court of Justice ruled that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya people in 2017. The court found an intent to destroy a large part of this minority group. This historic decision ends a legal battle that lasted seven years.
國際法院裁定緬甸於 2017 年對羅興亞人犯下種族滅絕罪。法院認定其意圖摧毀該少數族群的大部分人口。這項歷史性裁決結束了長達七年的法律訴訟。
💡 Why it matters

This is a historic ruling because the court has never held a state responsible for genocide before. It sets a critical standard for international human rights law.

這是一項歷史性判決，因為該法院此前從未認定任何國家須對種族滅絕負責。這為國際人權法樹立了重要標竿。
