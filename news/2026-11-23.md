## 1. Cop31 climate talks break down as key deal rejected
**Cop31 氣候談判破裂，關鍵協議遭否決**

來源：[The Guardian World](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/nov/22/cop31-climate-talks-set-to-end-in-deep-division-as-key-deal-rejected)

**摘要 Summary**

The EU and UK rejected a climate deal at the Cop31 talks in Turkey. They felt the deal did not address fossil fuels properly. Officials said the agreement failed to meet the needs of the current situation.

歐盟與英國在土耳其舉行的 Cop31 氣候談判中否決了一項協議。他們認為該協議未能妥善處理化石燃料問題。官員表示，這項協議未能滿足當前局勢的需求。

**為什麼重要 Why it matters**

This failure shows the difficulty of reaching global agreements on climate change.

這次談判失敗顯示了在氣候變遷議題上達成全球共識的困難度。

### 單字

- **reject** [rɪˈdʒɛkt] （v.）拒絕　✅ 多益常見字
  - 例句：The manager had to reject my request for a vacation.
  - 翻譯：經理不得不拒絕我的休假申請。
  - 同義詞：decline、refuse　搭配詞：reject a proposal、reject an offer
- **fail** [fel] （v.）失敗；未能做到　✅ 多益常見字
  - 例句：Do not fail to call me when you arrive home.
  - 翻譯：你到家時千萬別忘了打電話給我。
  - 同義詞：miss、lose　搭配詞：fail to do、fail a test
