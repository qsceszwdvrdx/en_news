輝達的季度營收達到 1158.4 億美元，是去年的兩倍。公司董事會表示不會讓前執行長黃仁勳復職。這份報告是在他被免職後不久發布的。

This shows how a major tech company performs financially even after a big leadership change.

This is a serious public health concern regarding disease control in the US.
