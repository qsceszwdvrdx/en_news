## 1. US supreme court clears way for Trump’s border wall in Texas’s Big Bend
**美國最高法院為川普在德州大彎曲國家公園興建邊境圍牆掃除障礙**

來源：[The Guardian World](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/09/us-supreme-court-border-wall-texas-big-bend)

**摘要 Summary**

The US supreme court allowed the government to continue building a wall in Texas. This decision stops a previous order that had paused the construction. The court believes border control is more important than environmental concerns.

美國最高法院允許政府繼續在德州興建圍牆。這項裁決停止了先前暫停施工的命令。法院認為邊境管制比環境問題更重要。

**為什麼重要 Why it matters**

This ruling shows the government's power to prioritize national security projects over local environmental rules.

這項裁決顯示政府有權將國家安全計畫置於當地環境法規之上。

### 單字

- **construction** [kənˈstrʌkʃən] （n.）建造，施工　✅ 多益常見字
  - 例句：The US supreme court has allowed Donald Trump to continue his administration’s construction of a border wall in Texas’s Big Bend region.
  - 翻譯：美國最高法院已允許唐納·川普繼續其政府在德州大彎曲地區的邊境圍牆興建工程。
  - 同義詞：building、development　搭配詞：under construction、construction site
- **suspend** [səˈspɛnd] （v.）暫停，中止　✅ 多益常見字
  - 例句：The nation’s highest court granted the Trump administration’s emergency request to suspend a lower court order that had temporarily stopped the project last month.
  - 翻譯：美國最高法院批准了川普政府的緊急請求，暫停了一項上個月曾暫時停止該計畫的下級法院命令。
  - 同義詞：pause、halt　搭配詞：suspend a project、suspend operations
