## 1. Congo Ebola outbreak reaches 12,000 cases as some responders evacuate
**剛果伊波拉疫情達1萬2千例，部分救援人員撤離**

來源：[NPR News](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/26/g-s1-155957/congo-ebola-responders)

**摘要 Summary**

An Ebola outbreak is spreading quickly in eastern Congo and has reached 12,000 cases. Fighting between rebels and the army makes it difficult for workers to stop the disease. Because of the danger, some responders have to evacuate.

伊波拉疫情在剛果東部快速蔓延，病例已達1萬2千例。叛軍與政府軍之間的衝突，讓工作人員難以控制疾病的擴散。由於局勢危險，部分救援人員不得不撤離。

**為什麼重要 Why it matters**

This outbreak is a serious global health issue because fighting makes medical help very dangerous.

這場疫情是嚴重的全球公共衛生問題，因為武裝衝突使得提供醫療援助變得非常危險。
