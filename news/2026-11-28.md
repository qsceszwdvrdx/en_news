## 1. Canada’s top court upholds Quebec law barring public sector workers from wearing religious symbols
**加拿大最高法院維持魁北克法律，禁止公部門員工配戴宗教象徵物**

來源：[The Guardian World](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/27/canadas-top-court-upholds-quebec-law-barring-public-sector-workers-from-wearing-religious-symbols)

**摘要 Summary**

Canada's top court decided to support a law in Quebec. This law stops government workers from wearing visible religious items at work. The decision shows a conflict between personal rights and government rules.

加拿大最高法院決定支持魁北克的一項法律。該法律禁止公部門員工在工作時配戴顯眼的宗教物品。這項裁決凸顯了個人權利與政府規範之間的衝突。

**為什麼重要 Why it matters**

This case is important because it balances personal religious freedom with government rules. It affects many public workers and sets an example for civil rights debates.

這起案件非常重要，因為它平衡了個人宗教自由與政府規範。這項決定影響了許多公部門員工，也為人權爭議樹立了判例。

### 單字

- **uphold** [ʌpˈhold] （v.）維持，支持（判決、決定）　✅ 多益常見字
  - 例句：The manager decided to uphold the company rule about late arrivals.
  - 翻譯：經理決定維持公司關於遲到的規定。
  - 同義詞：support、maintain　搭配詞：uphold a decision、uphold the law
- **sector** [ˈsɛktɚ] （n.）部門，行業　✅ 多益常見字
  - 例句：She works in the financial sector and travels often for business.
  - 翻譯：她在金融領域工作，經常因公出差。
  - 同義詞：area、part　搭配詞：public sector、private sector
- **challenge** [ˈtʃælɪndʒ] （n.）質疑，挑戰　✅ 多益常見字
  - 例句：Learning a new computer program was a big challenge for him.
  - 翻譯：學習一套新的電腦程式對他來說是一大挑戰。
  - 同義詞：difficulty、test　搭配詞：face a challenge、major challenge

## 2. OpenAI countersues Apple for allegedly stealing trade secrets
**OpenAI 反訴蘋果，指控其涉嫌竊取商業機密**

來源：[The Guardian World](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/27/openai-countersues-apple-trade-secrets)

**摘要 Summary**

OpenAI has filed a lawsuit against Apple in a federal court. It claims Apple stole trade secrets and stopped suppliers from working with OpenAI. This action comes after Apple first sued OpenAI over hiring employees.

OpenAI 已在聯邦法院對蘋果提起訴訟。該公司聲稱蘋果竊取商業機密，並阻礙供應商與 OpenAI 合作。在此之前，蘋果也曾針對挖角員工一事起訴 OpenAI。

**為什麼重要 Why it matters**

This lawsuit involves two major tech leaders fighting over important technology and workers. It could change how tech companies compete and protect their ideas.

這起訴訟涉及兩大科技巨頭在關鍵技術與人才上的爭奪。它可能會改變科技公司競爭以及保護技術創新的方式。

### 單字

- **lawsuit** [ˈlɔˌsut] （n.）訴訟，官司　✅ 多益常見字
  - 例句：The two partners settled their lawsuit before going to court.
  - 翻譯：兩位合夥人在上法庭之前解決了他們的訴訟。
  - 同義詞：suit、case　搭配詞：file a lawsuit、win a lawsuit
- **supplier** [səˈplaɪɚ] （n.）供應商　✅ 多益常見字
  - 例句：We need to find a new supplier for office paper.
  - 翻譯：我們需要為辦公用紙尋找新的供應商。

- **access** [ˈæksɛs] （n.）使用權限，存取途徑　✅ 多益常見字

  - 翻譯：晚上六點之後你有進入大樓的權限嗎？
  - 同義詞：entry、admission　搭配詞：have access to、gain access
