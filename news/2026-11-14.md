## 1. Microsoft outage: company scrambles to fix global issue that took down Outlook and Teams
**微軟系統大當機：公司緊急修復導致 Outlook 與 Teams 無法使用的全球性問題**

來源：[The Guardian World](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/13/microsoft-outage-outlook-teams-word-excel-reports)

**摘要 Summary**

Microsoft had a big technical problem that affected many users around the world. Popular services like Outlook and Teams stopped working for a while. The company apologized and said they have fixed the issue.

微軟發生了嚴重的技術問題，影響了全球許多用戶。Outlook 和 Teams 等熱門服務一度無法使用。公司已道歉並表示問題已經修復。

**為什麼重要 Why it matters**

This shows how much we rely on technology for daily work and communication. When big systems fail, it causes problems for businesses and public services.

這顯示了我們在日常工作與溝通中對科技的依賴程度。當大型系統故障時，會對企業與公共服務造成困擾。

### 單字

- **issue** [ˈɪʃu] （n.）問題　✅ 多益常見字
  - 例句：We need to discuss this issue at the next meeting.
  - 翻譯：我們需要在下次會議討論這個問題。
  - 同義詞：problem、matter　搭配詞：resolve an issue、technical issue
- **disruption** [dɪsˈrʌpʃən] （n.）中斷、混亂　✅ 多益常見字
  - 例句：The road construction caused a major disruption to traffic.
  - 翻譯：道路施工造成了嚴重的交通中斷。
  - 同義詞：interruption、disturbance　搭配詞：cause disruption、avoid disruption
